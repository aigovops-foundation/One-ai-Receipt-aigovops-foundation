# Adapter — aigovops-vendor-rfi

**Today.** Open-source vendor RFI and audit-training tool (Beacon × Umbrella), alpha.

**Delta.** RFI questions ask vendors for: the interface profiles they emit receipts on; the OR level an independent verifier reports (not the level they claim); which log operator and witnesses they use; whether `contestability` is present on consequential decisions; their privacy profile and reidentification risk assessment reference; their conflict disclosures. Answers are receipts and registry records, not prose; the tool verifies a sample with the reference verifier and shows claimed vs verified.
