# Release gates G0–G7

No gate may be passed by funding, by a single organisation, or by the Foundation alone.

| Gate | Passes when | Who confirms |
|---|---|---|
| **G0 · Seed** | v0.2 spec, schemas, reference signer/verifier, ≥ 10 vectors, TypeScript contract, governance drafts, delta matrix, adoption plan are public; CI green | Incubator |
| **G1 · Foundation emits and reads** | Beacon emits v0.2 behind a flag; Lantern shows claimed vs verified; the café shows a receipt id; a 400 verifies a stranger's receipt offline | Incubator + a practice host |
| **G2 · Governance live** | Charter, CoI register, funding policy, IP policy, code of conduct and decision process published; Public Interest Council seated and paid; first regional co-chairs named | Board + Council |
| **G3 · Interoperability** | Two independent implementations and two independent log/witness paths pass the vectors and cross-verify at a public event; SCITT -00 draft submitted | Incubator + Liaison Forum |
| **G4 · Pilots** | Three pilots (one public sector, one Global South) running with pre-registered evaluations; procurement clauses in one real contract; ten-language receipt view | Adoption Network + Council |
| **G5 · v0.3** | RFC 8785 canonicaliser and CBOR-CDE produce byte-identical signatures across implementations; OTel mapping and C2PA assertion submitted | Incubator |
| **G6 · Independent assurance** | Assessor eligibility and methodology public; first OR-4 records; at least one negative finding published; anti-equivocation drill passed | Council + Appeals Panel |
| **G7 · v1.0** | Layer-3 outcomes published for every pilot; registry stewardship transfer plan approved; Incubator ratifies with Council sign-off | Board, Incubator, Council |

Standing rules: no release claims standards status before ratification; no assurance claim above OR-2 ships without independent logging, anti-equivocation testing, contestability, privacy review and conflict disclosure; no certification program ever; no profile advances without at least one independent implementation from a non-sponsoring small organisation.
