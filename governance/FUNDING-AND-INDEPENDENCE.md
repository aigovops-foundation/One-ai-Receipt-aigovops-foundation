# Funding and independence policy

**Target.** $3.0M–$5.0M over the first eighteen months, diversified across commercial capacity sponsors, grants, public-interest funds, research funding and in-kind technical contribution — enough to pay for real independent work, small enough to avoid a bureaucracy before there is running code.

**Allocation.** Open technical core and security 25% · public-interest participation ≥ 20% (protected line) · independent research and evaluation 20% · pilots and adoption 15% · governance, legal and registry operations 10% · reserves and incident response 10%.

**Guardrails (binding).**
- No single contributor above 15% of annual unrestricted revenue; no commercial sector above 35%.
- At least 20% legally or contractually ring-fenced for compensated public-interest participation; at least 20% for independent evaluation and security/privacy research.
- Every contribution above $10,000 is publicly disclosed with amount or range, restrictions and conflicts.
- Restricted funding cannot determine a technical conclusion, assessor qualification, conformance outcome, public-interest review decision or publication result.
- Public-interest funding is multi-year where possible and never conditioned on favourable review.
- An annual transparency report covers revenues, expenses, in-kind contributions, governance participation, conflicts, implementation outcomes and unresolved dissent.

**Asymmetry by design.** Commercial participants finance shared technical capacity and independent oversight because they benefit from a credible, interoperable market. Public-interest, Indigenous, community, accessibility and independent research partners are compensated because their expertise is governance infrastructure, not volunteer decoration. Requests by partner group are in `COALITION-PLAYBOOK.md`.

**Sequence.** Listening invitation (45 minutes, no money) → one-page role-fit memo → non-binding letter of intent (`LOI-TEMPLATE.md`) → workplan and separate agreements for technical contribution, sponsorship, research grant, community participation grant and pilot data access.
