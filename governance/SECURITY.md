# Security policy

Report vulnerabilities privately through GitHub's private vulnerability reporting on this repository, or by e-mail to bob.rapp@aigovops.community and ken.johnston@aigovops.community, with affected version, reproduction, impact and suggested mitigation. Maintainers acknowledge within two business days, triage within seven calendar days, coordinate fixes and advisories, preserve reporter credit when requested, and never require public disclosure before a fix is available.

Cryptographic or privacy failures (a canonicalisation ambiguity, a forgeable signature, a linkability attack on commitments, a log equivocation) may trigger release suspension, key-revocation guidance and an `incident` receipt from the affected issuer. The demonstration keys in `examples/` are public by design and must never be used outside the vectors.
