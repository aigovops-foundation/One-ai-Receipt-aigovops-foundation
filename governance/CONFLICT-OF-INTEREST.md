# Conflict-of-interest policy

1. **Disclosure.** Every participant discloses beneficial ownership, employer, material commercial relationships with operators, log operators, assessors, insurers and registry stewards, and any funding received through the coalition. Disclosures are public in the conflict register.
2. **Recusal.** No one votes on a conformance, assessor-qualification, incident or appeal matter involving their own organisation, an affiliate, a client or a funder.
3. **Separation.** No organisation may assess, certify or operate the sole transparency log for an AI system it operates or materially controls at OR-3 and above. No provider chairs both the Technical Incubator and the Public Interest Council. No insurer chairs assessor qualification. No sponsor approves itself as an assessor.
4. **Assessors.** An assessor may not audit its own technology, related log service, controlled operator or underwriting client without full public disclosure and abstention; a public methodology and a negative-finding process are mandatory.
5. **Funding.** Commercial participants do not decide which civil-society organisations receive grants; restricted funding never determines a technical conclusion, assessor qualification, conformance outcome, public-interest decision or publication result.
6. **Marketing.** Participation is never marketed as certification, endorsement or standards status. No regulator name or logo is used as an endorsement.
7. **Enforcement.** The Public Interest Council hears conflict complaints; the Independent Appeals Panel hears appeals; outcomes and dissent are published.
