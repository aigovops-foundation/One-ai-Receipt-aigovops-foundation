# Charter — One Receipt incubation (v0.2)

## Purpose

To incubate an open, vendor-neutral, privacy-protective evidence layer for consequential AI transactions, and to hand it to a global collaborative in which no operator, log provider, assessor or insurer can grade its own commercial interest.

## Bodies

| Body | Purpose | Decision authority | Funding rule |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Governing Board** | Mission, fiduciary oversight, budget, host relationship | Cannot approve any member's conformance claim, assessor status or technical outcome | Publishes the annual transparency report |
| **Technical Incubator** (Technical Steering Committee) | Core specification, schemas, SDKs, verifier, test vectors, interoperability suite, registries, releases, working groups | Documented rough consensus; no member-specific veto; two non-author approvals for normative change | Contributions may support work, never purchase technical outcomes |
| **Public Interest Council** | Conflicts, privacy, accessibility, contestability, equity, data sovereignty, high-risk profile changes | **Binding** review and appeal authority over public-interest and conflict matters; may block or require revision of high-risk profiles | Paid participation from a protected budget line (≥ 20%); no single commercial funder controls it |
| **Adoption and Pilot Network** (Adoption Council) | Cross-sector pilots, procurement language, usability and evidence-outcome evaluation, chapters, education | Recommends profiles; publishes results; does not certify its own pilots | Pilots pay their own deployment cost and contribute anonymised learnings |
| **Independent Appeals Panel** | Conformance, conflict, suspension and procedural appeals | Final on process; may refer substance back with reasons | Members hold no commercial role in the coalition |
| **Standards Liaison Forum** | Reconciliation with SCITT, OVERT, OTel, C2PA, AP2, VC/DID, SDOs | Advises; no coalition claim of endorsement, adoption or standards status without the body's written decision | No cash ask of SDOs |
| **Public Interest Review Panel** | The OR-5 record: whether a system's contestability, privacy and community-authority commitments are real | Issues, suspends and withdraws OR-5 review records | Drawn from the Council; paid |

## Decision rules

Rough consensus in the Incubator, recorded in `docs/decisions/`; the Public Interest Council may return any change touching privacy, contestability, accessibility, community authority or conflicts with a binding request for revision; a 30-day public RFC precedes any breaking change; dissent is published, never suppressed; regulators participate as non-voting observers; at least half of non-vendor seats sit outside North America and the EU; regional co-chairs are named for every region with a pilot; ten working languages by Phase 2.

## Founding roles

The AiGovOps Foundation is the neutral convener, open-source maintainer and initial registry steward, with a written intent to transfer stewardship to a multi-party steward by Phase 3. It is not the long-term sole registry steward, assessor qualifier, notary operator or assurance issuer, and it never grades its own receipts.

Glacis and contributors to the OVERT community may be recognised as **proposed inaugural technical contributors** only after execution of a public founding-contribution instrument with conflict disclosure. Recognition is historical and non-governing: no veto, exclusivity, permanent seat, endorsement, trust anchor, assessor status or certification privilege follows from it.

## Reserved protections

Commercial funding purchases capacity, never votes, trust status, certification, technical outcomes or procurement preference. No contributor above 15% of unrestricted annual revenue; no sector above 35%; at least 20% for compensated public-interest participation; at least 20% for independent research, privacy and security evaluation. Operators, log operators, witnesses, assessors and registry stewards disclose beneficial ownership and material relationships and recuse from their own matters.
